# Title

Give the architectural decision a short, descriptive title.

## Status

State the status: Proposed, Accepted, Rejected, Deprecated, or Superseded.

## Date

Record the decision date in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Context

Describe the problem, goals, and constraints that require a decision.

## Decision

State the chosen approach and why it fits the context.

## Alternatives considered

List the other approaches considered and why they were not chosen.

## Consequences

Describe the benefits, tradeoffs, risks, and follow-up work.
